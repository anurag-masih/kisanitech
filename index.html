About the Project

Kisani is developing an AI-powered precision weed management platform that uses computer vision and robotics to reduce herbicide use, lower labor costs, and improve agricultural sustainability. This open-source MVP acts as our autonomous pipeline: SEE → THINK → MOVE → TARGET.

👁️ Vision & AI

Processes overhead plot imagery via browser-based computer vision to accurately identify target weeds.

📐 Coordinate Mapping

Instantly translates camera pixel coordinates into real-world millimeter coordinates ($X, Y$) for gantry positioning.

⚙️ Direct G-Code Execution

Dynamically compiles movement commands and laser/tool parameters, streaming directly to GRBL-based hardware via USB.
